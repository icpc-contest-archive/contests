# No-sources hunt, 2026-11-16

One search agent per contest for the 90 catalogue contests that had no sources at all. Nothing in the catalogue was changed. Full per-contest results (URLs, roles, leads, what was searched) are in `_hunt/nosrc-2026-11-16/results-all.json` in the icpc-contests folder.

## Outcome

| Outcome | Contests |

| Nothing found | 15 |

36 of the 90 now have a results source (scoreboard, standings or rankings). 62 have at least one source that is more than an existence record.

## Catalogue corrections the agents propose

- vietnam-2026 is in Da Nang, not Ho Chi Minh City (CMS 9683 renamed; slug still HCMC-2027).
- lac-2027 is in Monterrey, Mexico; nac-2027 is at BYU, not Orlando; euc-2027 is in Eindhoven.
- taiwan-ncpc-2016 is misfiled: it is the 1st National Technology Universities contest at National Formosa University, and CMS 3076 is a different contest (6th National Private Universities contest, Tatung University).
- taiwan-1994 was probably a Taiwan-only selection, not a joint Asia regional; taiwan-1993 may need the same check.
- erc: pre-1988 editions were British (Imperial College won 1984 and 1985; 1987 was in London). erc-1983 remains unconfirmed.
- rmc: the contemporary name was "ACM Mountain Region Programming Contest"; "Metro State Denver" is unconfirmed as a site. The "1990 Mountain Region" problem set filed under rmc-1990 is probably the autumn-1989 set (season-year naming).
- malaysia-national-2014 host was Universiti Teknologi PETRONAS; philippines-national-2015 final was 2015-10-19 in Tuguegarao.
- turkey: a 2025 edition exists (inzva report), which the catalogue says was not held.
- dhaka-2026 has a second CMS record (9657); lethbridge-2022's only source is the Alberta contest announcement; kharagpur-2020's standings URL is the 2019 contest.
- Several "web" sources of neighbouring editions are front pages now showing 2026 (kanpur, amppz, nac, euc, romania-2025's two URLs, acm-icpc.olp.vn).

## Limits

- The Wayback Machine was blocked for every agent: no snapshots were checked or made.
- Figures such as winners and team counts often came through a page summariser; check against the raw page before recording.
- About 20 contests are genuinely upcoming and need a re-check after their date (see the Date column).
- The 2026 Pakistan mock problem set PDF contains a hidden instruction aimed at AI tools (an anti-cheating trap); ignore it when reading that file.
