a new series, `tehran-online` (subregional). QOJ files it under 2023; its id is 2022 because it
belongs to the delayed 2022 cycle (regional on 27-28 April 2023). The QOJ import skips the same
kind of contest elsewhere (Asia East online qualifiers, Taiwan Online Programming Contest) as
"not a catalogue contest", so this one is now the exception: keep it, or drop it, or add those.

and the first phase's problems (14 sets). The merge report has the table and a proposed repair.

# Second round, same day

Answers to "Open after this round" above, and five things found on `alberta-2017`.

## Repair the Brazil finals — yes

`tools/repair_brazil_finals.py`, report in `brazil-finals-repair-2026-11-12.md`. For 2005-2018 the
mixed set is split: the first phase's problems and Baekjoon page go to `brazil-first-phase-<year>`
(12 sets made, 2 extended), the final joins `latam-<year>` with the letters its own regional page
gives. `latam-2005`, `-2006` and `-2014` are new; 2006 and 2014 have no problem list yet. The
catalogue had no first-phase contest for 2005-2007: three are added, without dates. The 2019 and
2023 first-phase Baekjoon pages moved too. 1,280 sets before, 1,275 after.

## tehran-online — arguments asked for

Given in the conversation; no change yet.

## Upcoming contests whose date has passed — yes, and search the usual places

`tools/apply_ran_2026.py`, report in `autumn-2026-2026-11-12.md`. 40 contests are no longer
upcoming (102 before, 62 now) and 69 sources were added: ICPC standings pages where the CMS holds
ranked standings (27 contests), and last year's addresses with the year moved on where they
answered (Kattis, DOMjudge archives, scorelatam, contest sites). Six have a past date and nothing
that shows they ran: azerbaijan, nanjing, qatar, romania, torneo-chileno, turkey. Codeforces could
not be read this round.

## Found on alberta-2017, fixed everywhere

- **Divisions.** Sources get `scope: Division 1` / `Division 2`, shown beside the role. 169 sources
  on 10 series, from the archived page (Kattis filter heading, title), the address or the note
  (`tools/label_scopes.py`).
- **Letters.** A problem gets the one letter left when the set is complete and it is the one problem
  left (`tools/fill_letters.py`): alberta-2017 B, japan-2000 H, mausa-2016 B. 105 letter maps still
  miss problems and are not guessed.
- **Administrative sites** are never shown; the data keeps them. A contest with no real site shows
  the city its CMS record points at, marked "inferred".
- **"N raw captures in the archive repo"** is gone from contest pages: every capture is listed on
  its source's page, and the line linked into a folder that only exists on a machine running the
  local archive server.
- **Random page.** `Random` in the header, or the key r, opens a page picked at random (kind first,
  then page); `random.html` lets a kind be chosen.
