| tehran-online-2022 | 2023-03-16 | icpc.sharif.edu/2022: 19th Iran Internet Programming Contest | see below |

The Tehran contest is the online qualifier before the regional, not a second regional. It gets
a new series, `tehran-online` (subregional). QOJ files it under 2023; its id is 2022 because it
belongs to the delayed 2022 cycle (regional on 27-28 April 2023). The QOJ import skips the same
kind of contest elsewhere (Asia East online qualifiers, Taiwan Online Programming Contest) as
"not a catalogue contest", so this one is now the exception: keep it, or drop it, or add those.

## 3. Merge the duplicate problem sets — yes

`tools/merge_problemsets.py`, report in `problemset-merge-2026-11-12.md`. 87 sets became 20:
`latam-<year>` for 2007-2013 and 2015-2025, `east-na-ecna-2025`, `scusa-seusa-2020`. Old set and
problem addresses redirect (793 redirects). 1,347 sets before, 1,280 after.

Two of the 22 groups in the review were wrong: `brazil-2019` and `brazil-2023` did not share a
set with the first phase, they carried the first phase's list by mistake. Their rows moved to
the first-phase sets and the two finals now use the regional's set.

Found on the way and left alone: the Brazil finals of 2005-2018 carry a mix of the regional's
and the first phase's problems (14 sets). The merge report has the table and a proposed repair.
