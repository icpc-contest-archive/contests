# Site QA, 2026-11-16: what was asked, what changed, what is open

Fredrik reviewed 17 random pages. Every point was traced to the generator or the data and fixed
for the whole site, not for the page. Nothing is committed or pushed.

## Rulings taken from the review

- A source is an address at a point in time. One address meant at two times is two sources, each
  with its own page, linking to the other.
- Of our copies only the current one is shown. Earlier copies stay in the archive for debugging.
- Notes and comments are not shown anywhere on the site: source notes, contest, series, set,
  problem and location notes, the evidence text of a check, "data from", "rendered from".
- Internal ids are not shown (source id, judge id). The link to the data repository is gone.
- A source page states facts as linked data, not sentences: type, contest, problem set, problem.
- One vocabulary and one set of colours for the three places a source is found.
- "Online judge" is the word in every label and heading, never "judge" alone.
- A problem set whose contests all used the same letters needs no "Letters per contest" section.

## The three places: names, words, colours

Proposed and built; Fredrik asked for a proposal, so this is his to confirm or change.

| place | green: it is there | amber: there, in doubt | red: confirmed not there | blue: not done yet | grey: does not apply |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| address (the address now) | live | changed, blocked, unverified, empty | dead, replaced | not checked | |
| Wayback (the Wayback Machine) | snapshot | bad snapshot | no snapshot, excluded | not checked | n/a |
| archive (our archive) | held | bad copy | lost | not captured | n/a |

- The label of a mark is the place and never changes. It is never struck through. The colour is the answer.
- Amber means something is there but a person has to look. Blue means work of ours that is pending.
  Grey means there is nothing to do: the Wayback Machine for a CMS standings page (an app, a
  snapshot shows nothing), our archive for an empty page.
- The same colours now mean the same thing in every pill, mark, bar and square. Red (lost) is new in
  the coverage palette. The card at the top of a contest, a problem set and a source is one component.

## What changed in the data

- **Per-problem sources**: `problem: <letter>` replaces the note "problem A" on 156 SEERC sources. The
  source page links contest, problem set and problem through it.
- **Problem count and letters from scoreboards**: 81 contests that had nothing recorded about their
  problems now have a count and letters, read off the header of a scoreboard we hold; 3 Latin America
  sites joined `latam-2026`. Contests with no problem list fell from 398 to 347. Ten contests with a
  held scoreboard are still without a count: two have division scoreboards that disagree, eight are in
  a form the reader does not know. `reports/scoreboard-letters-2026-11-16.md`.
- **"changed" verdicts**: 80 of 426 addresses judged changed are live. The check compared whole pages,
  so a Kattis scoreboard with an unchanged table and new site dressing scored 0.53. Comparing the
  tables gives 0.98. rmc-2021 is one of them. Fixed in the data and in the capture tool.
- **Stale "incomplete" flag**: a list as long as its count now reads complete whatever the flag says.

## Counts asked for

**Problem set documents we hold a copy of** (type problem set, not on an online judge, recorded as
holding statements). "Full, proper, official" is not checked beyond that record.

| format | sources | contests |
| --- | --- | --- |
| PDF | 813 | 798 |
| web page | 537 | 463 |
| zip, rar, gzip archive | 58 | 56 |
| crawled website | 15 | 15 |
| PostScript | 6 | 7 |

| plain text | 3 | 3 |
| Word | 2 | 2 |

1,251 contests have at least one. Another 136 held PDFs have not been read for what they hold.
Separately, 158 single-problem statement PDFs (SEERC).

**Online judges**: 28 registered, 27 active and one closed. The sweep of 2026-11-14 found matches on
eight dead judges (HIT 275, BZOJ 179, CSU 97, FZU 71, HRBUST 54, HihoCoder 42, UESTC 12) and two
re-hosts (UESTC at cdoj.site 147, HUST 75) and left them out because a link would go nowhere. UVa was
not covered by the sweep.

**Shared sets and letters**: 73 sets are shared by several contests. In 69 two or more letterings are
recorded; 57 are identical, 12 differ. In 11 of the 12 the contests did not sit the same problems
(Latin America sites with fewer problems listed, the South NA divisions). One pair sat exactly the same
problems under different letters: ecna-2023 and nena-2023.

**Statements where an online judge has the problems**: 115 sets read "statements: link only". The
statements exist on the judge; what we hold is the judge's list page, not each problem's page. It is
not a doubt about whether they are official.

## Copies without their stylesheets and images

Of 6,991 pages on the public replay site, 1,649 lack at least one stylesheet (938 lack all of them)
and 1,816 lack images. The asset pass took the first 25 references in document order (12 from the
Wayback Machine), so icons, fonts and scripts used up the allowance before the main stylesheet.
`capture_urls.py` 2.20 takes stylesheets first, then images, scripts last, never site icons, and
`--assets-redo` revisits captures taken the old way.
