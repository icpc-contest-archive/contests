## Open

1. **Trial-session problems.** The organisers published them next to the contest problems: 1998
   (Brackets, Permutations), 2003 (X Spływ), 2005 (X zeB ikjab, Y ikjab zeB, Z Sudoku), 2006 (X zeB
   ikjaB, Y ikjaB zeB, Z Żarówki). Only the two of 1998 are in the catalogue, because SPOJ lists
   them under the contest's source string; they carry a note and the set's count of 9 includes
   them. The schema has no way to say "trial problem". Decide: record them (with a flag) or not.
2. **1996 problem G**: title unknown. Poznań (prof. Nawrocki's group) may still have it.
3. **Statements are available for capture** for every year listed with a set: PostScript (1998,
   2001), Word (1997), HTML with solutions (1996), PDF (2002-2006), plus tests for 1998, 2001, 2002
   and model solutions and tests for 2005. None of it is in the problem archive yet.
4. **2002 also published five problems of the Wrocław internal qualifier** (Kadrowa, k-RSA, Mrówki,
   Pionek, Wykopki). Not an ICPC contest; not added.
5. **Szkopuł** (szkopul.edu.pl/task_archive/amppz/) carries AMPPZ 2011-2014 and 2023 as solvable
   problems (59 in all). It is not in `judges.yaml` yet.
