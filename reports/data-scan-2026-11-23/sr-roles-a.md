Per the brief ("remove when the address never served that edition; keep as lost when it was the contest's site but no copy exists"), the answer depends on whether the address was ever the edition's site. Best argument for removal: a role-less source is invalid and the page is demonstrably not the edition. Recommendation: remove the ones that never served the edition (Usenet, Timus, Kattis "No data", GitHub, news sites, general university/online-judge sites), keep as lost with a fresh capture the ones that were the organisers' address (icpccaribe.org, ikit.sfu-kras.ru, neerc.ifmo.ru subregion pages, cs.uleth.ca LCPC). Recommendation: `web` only for pages with real content about the edition (statistics, news list); no role (remove) for pure link menus and third-party indexes; keep submission logs only if a role for run logs is wanted (none exists: they are neither scoreboard nor problemset).

**D3. Link-index pages as `problemset`/`editorial` (9 keep, ~12 more).** I kept `problemset` for pages whose job is to hand out the contest's problems as files (bapc-2017 and bapc-2023 Problems pages, india-2016 category page, japan-2011 Problems page, kanpur-2001 x2, ETH archive pages cerc-1995/96/97 where I did apply `problemset`), and kept `editorial` for japan-2019 "Commentaries and Data from Judges". Basis: describe_rubric lists "a page of links to files" among the forms of a problem set. The second reading said `[]` for most of these and `web` for bapc-2023/japan-2022. Question: does a link page count as the problem set, given the linked files are separate sources (bapc-2017 has `files/problems.pdf` as its own source)? For: matches the "judge's contest page" precedent and the ETH archive pages. Against: double counting and a page that holds no problem. Recommendation: keep `problemset` where the page is the contest's problem page and the files are not separate sources; where files are separate sources, `web` or nothing. Pages that link only program/information sheets (japan-2019/2022 Contest Documents, mcpc-2003/06/07 standings link pages, korea-2023 notice, japan-2014 Teams "See the list here") are not problem sets: `web` or nothing (LINK-ONLY list in data.json, 7 items).
