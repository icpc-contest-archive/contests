# dates-b: contest dates, regions Latin America / Africa & Arab / Asia Pacific / Asia West / Asia East

Rule used (same as dates-a): `date` = the day the contest itself ran, not the first day of a multi-day event, not registration/practice/opening/excursion day. CMS dates were used only as a clue (CMS carries the first/registration day).

## What I checked (rerunnable)
- Inputs: `data/describe/run1-2026-11-16/contest-facts.json`, fact "date", not applied, in my regions: 95 "same" (nothing to do), 115 conflicts, 6 fills-blank. Plus the 114 contests of these regions with no `date` (listed below).
- Method: read each fact's `seen` text (the description-round reading of a held document) and applied the dates-a rule: a single explicit contest day (problem-set cover, scoreboard start stamp, schedule line "Programming Contest", report "final round was held on") is applied; spans ("9 e 10 de novembro", "5 - 6 November") and anything where two documents disagree are held back. Weekday sanity checked (`work/t2.py` prints doc/catalogue/CMS dates with weekdays).
- Scripts: `work/load.py`, `work/t2.py`, `work/mkops.py` (builds ops.json and data.json from the fact list).
- LIMIT: I was interrupted once and had limited time. I did not re-open the held copies behind each reading (dates-a did spot checks for its regions); the applied values rest on the description-round reading of the primary document, with a second source only where the reading itself cites one (e.g. Jakarta run-down page + scoreboard stamp, Taiwan schedule pages, Kattis start stamps). The hunt over the 114 blank-date contests was NOT done beyond the fills-blank facts; a first scan of the held NZPC copies found no dated text.
