# dates-a: contest dates, regions North America / Europe / Northern Eurasia / International

Rule used: `date` = the day the contest itself ran (not the first day of a multi-day event, not registration/practice/opening/closing day).

## What I checked (rerunnable)
- Inputs: `data/describe/run1-2026-11-16/contest-facts.json` (fact "date", not applied, class conflict / fills-blank; 394 entries in my regions, 265+9 are "same" and need nothing), `span-dates-nulled.json`, the span list in `reports/source-descriptions-2026-11-21.md`, and every contest of these regions without a date (244, of which 13 are upcoming).
- For each conflict I read the description-round evidence, then checked a second source where one existed: official ICPC news pages (news.icpc.global/wf*), cphof.org, live problem-set PDFs (seerc.icpc.global), the NSTU contest list (olymp.nstu.ru/contests, which dates every West Siberian ACM contest), Kattis boards, FAMNIT / FRI / MIMUW news, Wayback copies, the Usenet copy for ecna-1994, plus weekday sanity (regionals run Sat/Sun, weekday contests are noted).
- CMS dates were used as a clue only. Over 703 dated contests, the catalogue equals the CMS date in 487, CMS+1 day in 124, CMS+2 in 55: CMS carries the first (registration) day, so a CMS date that is earlier than the document date is not a conflict.
- Helper scripts: `work/verify.py`, `work/mkops.py`, `work/scan_nodate.py`, `work/gym.py`.

## Items for discussion with Fredrik

1. **Rule for contests whose documents give only a span of days** (the 8 in "Decisions waiting" 2; 7 are in my regions: ecna-1993, ecna-1994, east-siberia-2003/2006/2007, croatia-2014, romania-2026; kuwait-2026 is not mine).
   - Question: what goes in `date` when only "5-6 November" is known?
   - Options. (a) *Last day of span*: simple, and what 54 of the 75 already-dated span contests use; but wrong whenever the event ends with a closing/excursion day (East Siberia, CERC, SEERC 1999) and it silently invents precision. (b) *First day*: wrong for events that open with registration/practice (7 nena-atlantic contests and poland-2006 currently carry the first day for exactly this reason). (c) *Main-round day, found from any source (schedule, results header, Usenet post); blank if none*: matches the "day the contest ran" rule and the East Siberia series (explicit main rounds on the middle day in 2010/2016/2018/2019; catalogue 2008-2013 uses the middle day); costs research and leaves some blank. (d) Add a separate `date_end` / span field and keep `date` blank only when the main day is unknown.
   - Recommendation: (c) with (d) for the leftovers. Done under (c): ecna-1994 -> 1994-11-12 (Usenet results posted that Saturday night), romania-2026 -> 2026-11-08 (results sheet headed 8 Nov). Still blank: ecna-1993 (candidate 11-06 by analogy with 1994), east-siberia-2003/2006/2007 (candidate = middle day), croatia-2014 (two rounds 19 and 26 Oct: which is the contest?).
   - Full table of the 75 span contests with the relation of the catalogue date to the span: data.json `span_contests`. Odd ones: ctuo-1996 (span 26-27 Apr, catalogue 1996-05-04), the six nena-atlantic.
2. **Why the catalogue was one day late.** Most of the changes are the contest day vs the last day of the event (SEERC Sunday vs Saturday, WF day after, Poland Monday). I treated the documents as right wherever they name the contest day. If you would rather keep "last day of the event" for some series, say so before applying. Recommendation: apply as proposed.
3. **Local rounds on different days** (bapc-prelims-2021: most 9 Oct, Groningen and Mons 16 Oct; bapc-prelims-2025: 20, 26, 27 Sep; urals-2003 rounds 23 and 25 Apr; croatia-2014). One `date` cannot be the contest day. Options: the main/first-listed day (CMS agrees: 2021-10-09, 2025-09-20), the last, or leave blank with a span note. Recommendation: the day most teams played (bapc-prelims-2021 -> 2021-10-09), blank for urals-2003 until the championship round is identified.
4. **nena-2000**: the host's dates page and the Baylor standings heading say the Regional Final was Mon 6 Nov 2000; the rules page and CMS say Sat 4 Nov. Catalogue (11-04) left. Option for 11-06: two sources agree; against: Monday is unusual and the same site's older page says 4 Nov. Recommendation: ask whoever knows NENA 2000, otherwise 2000-11-06.
5. **Catalogue has no dates for ~216 contests that ran** (list in data.json `no_date_remaining`): mostly 1979-2007 subregions (socal, scusa, seusa, rmc, ncna, ecna 1984-92, NEERC subregions 1998-2007) with no held source that states a day, plus slovenia 2009/2010/2016/2017/2022, hungary, lethbridge, turkey, nena-* 2011-2018 (CMS-only dates). Candidates are in data.json `candidates_not_applied`. Recommendation: do not fill from CMS alone (it is the registration day); a targeted hunt for the NEERC subregion families (neerc.ifmo.ru archive pages) is the most productive.
6. **Upcoming contests that have passed**: west-siberia-2026 ran on 2026-11-21 (date set; its `status: upcoming` is for the structure agent); the other 12 upcoming have no date and I did not look for future dates.
7. Not a date problem but noticed: ecna-2023's old front page announced Sat 21 Oct 2023, while the contest was the NA East Division on 2023-10-29 (set).
