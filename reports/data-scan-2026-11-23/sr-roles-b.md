Question: what do we do with a `web` source whose held copy is a general site that never mentions the edition? Options: (1) remove the source (it never served the edition), (2) keep it as lost where the address was the contest's site (rulings 2026-11-14: "keep as lost when the address was the contest's site but no copy exists"), (3) set roles [] and keep for provenance. Best argument for (2): `www.cs.rit.edu/~icpc` really was the NENA site in the early years, the held copy is a 2026 redirect to the department; for sites like `fpmi.bsu.by`, `nmcabuja.org`, `acm.xidian.edu.cn`, `sp.urfu.ru`, `t.me/icpcuz` the address was never the contest's. Examples: nena-2005, nena-2010..2015 (RIT), west-neerc-2010..2020 (BSU faculty site), seerc-2013 (VNTU photo gallery), nigeria-2019. Recommendation: per address, judged by whether the contest's own pages existed there (url-hunt notes); list in data.json (action discuss, why starting "GROUP A"; also GROUP E for personal/history/ministry pages, GROUP G for Perl/GNU manuals mirrored on the contest site scusa-2012/2014).
